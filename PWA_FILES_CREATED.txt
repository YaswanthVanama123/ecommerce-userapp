================================================================================
CACHING STRATEGIES
================================================================================

Network-First (Fresh Data):
- /api/auth/me (User data)
- /api/cart (Shopping cart)
- /api/orders (Orders)
- /api/products/featured (Featured products)

Cache-First (Speed):
- /api/products (Product listings)
- Static assets (JS, CSS, fonts)
- Images (7-day cache)

Stale-While-Revalidate:
- HTML pages
- Navigation requests

Cache Expiration:
- API: 5 minutes
- Images: 7 days
- Static: 30 days
